## Assessment

The failure rate rose to 2.0% from 1.5%, but GitHub's release downloads, its artifact API outage on 11-11 and Dependabot caused 53 of the 68 failures on `main`. Release-asset downloads returned 5xx on 31 jobs, 8 of them on `main` and 22 in four runs started together on `instance-from-name` at 05:45Z on 11-09, and the outage failed 21 jobs on `main` at their artifact upload or download. All 24 Dependabot failures on `main` are real breaks. Besides the standing `got` patch and k8s bump failures (7 each), Dependabot's automatic security updates for `/dashboard` have failed since 11-10, 9 times this week, because the dashboard's dependencies hold 12 vulnerable packages below their fixed versions. Of the other 15 failures on `main`, 9 passed on a re-run of the same commit, but one of those, `cli-rate-limiter-deadline-exit-code`, is a bug in rdd that the re-run only hid. The new `image-pull-request-settled-timeout` failed once on `main` and once on PR #1226, which changes only the kube reconciler, so that PR's diff probably didn't cause it. In the job whose rdd log we read, the call that starts the pull returned after about 33 s, 0.2 s before the test gave up.

<details>
<summary>How this report is built</summary>

`ci-sweep weekly --week 2026-W46` over `sweep.db`, one row per **failed job** — a run can fail on one matrix leg out of many, so the job is the unit.

- **Known flake** — a job whose log matched a `matchers.yaml` pattern. Pattern matching only, no LLM.
- **One flake per job** — when several matchers fire, the job counts under the one whose surface appears **earliest** in the log, because the first failure is the root and what follows is fallout: a suite killed at its wall-clock cap logs a failed wait on the way out. The others are recorded but not counted, and only a true tie is left uncounted.
- **Proven flake** — the identical job passed on a re-run of the same commit, so the code cannot be at fault.
- **Unrecognized** — no matcher fired. On `main` it earns analysis; on a PR branch it earns only a count, because PR failures are dev breakage, not CI health.
- **No log** — the log expired or was never uploaded, so the sweep could not classify the failure. A data gap, not a clean bill.
- **What failed** — for a known flake, the matcher's summary; otherwise the failing test and command, or the job error for a failure that never reached its tests. `--fetch` names the rest, at one download each.
- Counts cover only jobs the sweep recorded. A period never swept reads as zero failures.

</details>
