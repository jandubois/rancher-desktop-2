Acknowledged Limitations ¶

  • Round 1 recorded two consequences of #917's rejection policy as the author's call, and this round leaves both unchanged. On removal, a file holding our block plus a stray marker is rejected, so our export PATH line stays live on the paths that do not retry (rdd svc delete and App deletion). Under front or back, a block followed by a stray marker reports Malformed on every pass, so Settled stays False until the user edits the file.
  • Round 1 dropped sizing the after slice to the remaining lines, because a startup file has a few dozen lines. Do not re-suggest it.
