## Assessment

Failed jobs fell to 62 from 117 and the failure rate fell to 0.8% from 1.8%, mostly because the gvisor-tap-vsock 0.9.0 bump (#1007), which failed 50 jobs in W43, merged on 10-22. Nothing that merged broke `main`, and no fixed flake came back. Dependabot accounts for 17 of the 31 failures on `main`, 12 of them from two standing breaks, the npm `got` patch (waiting on yarnpkg/berry#7282) and the k8s.io/kubernetes 1.37.1 bump, which can't resolve while `/rdd` replaces the staging modules at v0.36.4. Lost runners took 10 of the 13 BATS jobs on `main` and 14 of 572 Windows BATS jobs overall (2.4%, against 3.7% in W43 and 2.9-3.0% in the two weeks before), but one lower week doesn't show an improvement, so they are still the failure to root-cause. The next fix is `.github/workflows/rdd-bats.yaml:159`, where nothing checks the exit code of `pacman.exe`, so the MSYS2 mirror stall on 10-26 went unnoticed on all four Windows legs until `bats-others` failed on it. The `nerdctl build` row that spans three branches isn't a flake, because #1118 to #1120 are a single stack that one push broke with an `lchown` error and the next push fixed.

<summary>How this report is built</summary>

`ci-sweep weekly --week 2026-W44` over `sweep.db`, one row per **failed job** — a run can fail on one matrix leg out of many, so the job is the unit.

- **Known flake** — a job whose log matched a `matchers.yaml` pattern. Pattern matching only, no LLM.
- **One flake per job** — when several matchers fire, the job counts under the one whose surface appears **earliest** in the log, because the first failure is the root and what follows is fallout: a suite killed at its wall-clock cap logs a failed wait on the way out. The others are recorded but not counted, and only a true tie is left uncounted.
- **Proven flake** — the identical job passed on a re-run of the same commit, so the code cannot be at fault.
- **Unrecognized** — no matcher fired. On `main` it earns analysis; on a PR branch it earns only a count, because PR failures are dev breakage, not CI health.
- **No log** — the log expired or was never uploaded, so the sweep could not classify the failure. A data gap, not a clean bill.
- **What failed** — for a known flake, the matcher's summary; otherwise the failing test and command, or the job error for a failure that never reached its tests. `--fetch` names the rest, at one download each.
- Counts cover only jobs the sweep recorded. A period never swept reads as zero failures.

</details>
